Nothing here is drawn. The plate is binarised, thinned to a one-pixel spine and walked into lengths of centreline, cut at every crossing; the figures are those lengths untouched, and every letter is the same lengths snipped at a stated point along their arc and put somewhere else. The 1 is an n. The 10's bowl is a b. The 0 is an o, and its right-hand half is a D. The 7 is two thirds of a z. Every piece is then a Verlet rod with length, bend and home constraints — hover the word to shove it, cut loose to drop the bend memory. No outlines anywhere: each link is a capsule going through the same field renderer as the creatures, so the blur is the engine's own smooth-min, not a filter.
